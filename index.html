GET TO KNOW US

About Our Department

The Department of Commerce at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, focuses on providing students with a strong foundation in commerce, business and financial education.

The department encourages academic learning, analytical thinking, professional development and participation in extracurricular activities.

Through classroom learning, discussions, seminars and practical exposure, students are encouraged to develop the knowledge and skills required in today's business environment.

Our Vision

To encourage academic excellence, critical thinking and responsible leadership among students.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and create opportunities for students to develop their academic, analytical and professional abilities.
